# Hand-drawn visual set

Shared direction: minimalist hand-drawn startup doodles, imperfect black monoline ink, naive and slightly goofy proportions, white background, generous whitespace, and one sparse IKB blue `#002FA7` accent. No gradients, shadows, textures, realism, logos, watermarks, or embedded labels.

1. Name: Can AI spot a great business?
   Use: Opening image or title section.
   Prompt: A cute tiny AI robot uses a magnifying glass to inspect a sturdy small business while holding an annual report.
   Notes: Square composition; keep the business and robot equally legible.

2. Name: Pitch filtering funnel
   Use: Dataset-preparation section after introducing the VIC pitches.
   Prompt: A messy mountain of investment-pitch papers enters a large funnel; a tiny robot sorts the small clean stack that emerges.
   Notes: The volume contrast should be obvious without numerical labels.

3. Name: Providers disagree
   Use: The MCEM 1,724x data-error section.
   Prompt: Two expressive price-chart clipboards disagree, one rising absurdly and one modestly, while a confused calculator investigates with a magnifying glass.
   Notes: Use blue only on the suspicious point or line.

4. Name: Blocking hindsight
   Use: Point-in-time financial-data and leakage section.
   Prompt: A little AI robot stands behind a calendar boundary while future documents with wings wait on the other side.
   Notes: Make the time boundary the blue accent.

5. Name: The training run
   Use: Qwen3 LoRA and Colab section.
   Prompt: A tiny robot turns a hand crank attached to a laptop while a wobbly loss line slopes downward and a few loose bolts fall out.
   Notes: The scene should feel functional but uncertain, not triumphant.

6. Name: The boring baseline wins
   Use: Evaluation section beside the 15% versus 30.9% result.
   Prompt: A simple pencil-and-ruler character calmly crosses a race finish before an over-equipped surprised robot.
   Notes: Keep the mood humorous rather than humiliating.

7. Name: Human plus AI judgment
   Use: Practical-use section near the end of the blog.
   Prompt: A human investor and a friendly robot sort evidence together; the robot points out an overlooked card while the human holds the final decision stamp.
   Notes: Accent the overlooked evidence card in blue.

8. Name: The research loop
   Use: Closing summary or social-post final image.
   Prompt: A circular loop connects a pitch paper, filing folder, chart inspection, AI robot, and test finish flag while a human hand steers from the center.
   Notes: Use one blue arrow segment to suggest iteration.
